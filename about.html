About us

Lighter systems for persistent ISR.

Atlas AeroSystems is developing stratospheric paragliders for persistent intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance.

Our mission

We want to make long-duration ISR more efficient. Our approach is a textile wing: by replacing rigid structure with fabric, we aim to reduce the weight and volume of the system so it is easier to carry, transport, and deploy.

The wing is being developed primarily for special operations forces, and can also serve the Army, the Marine Corps, and border security.

Where we are

Atlas AeroSystems was founded on December 8, 2025. We are in the early research and prototype phase.

Our founders bring backgrounds in aeronautical engineering, guidance, navigation and control, parachute systems, systems engineering, and manufacturing.

Our team

Distributed across the Midwest.

We don’t work from a single headquarters. Our founders are based across four Midwest cities.
